Short essays on applying modernist principles to interface design. They are the reasoning behind the rules in the [documentation](/less-is-more-ui/docs/), written for people who have to make a decision on a real screen.

- [Less is more, applied to UI](/less-is-more-ui/notes/less-is-more-applied-to-ui/) — reduction as a test, not a style.
- [The silent grid](/less-is-more-ui/notes/the-silent-grid/) — why alignment beats borders.
- [Colour as signal, not decoration](/less-is-more-ui/notes/color-as-signal/) — the loudest element should carry meaning.
- [The subtractive pass](/less-is-more-ui/notes/the-subtractive-pass/) — a three-step review for any interface.

New notes are added when a decision needs more than a rule can carry.
